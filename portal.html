Metric definitions & provenance

Package responsibilities

QuantStats is the sole return-metric engine. Plotly renders interactive charts. The backtest engine supplies commission- and slippage-adjusted equity and trade records. ARCH supplies EGARCH estimates; rejected fits remain unavailable.

PyPortfolioOpt is an allocation engine. No portfolio weights, common base-currency valuation or investable implementation have been defined here, so optimized allocations and portfolio ratios are not published. Pyfolio tear sheets are not used as a second or replacement calculation engine.

Strict data contract

EODHD daily observations are the only market-data input. Missing prices, OHLC and volume are never fabricated, interpolated, resampled or replaced by another symbol or provider. Cache stores prior EODHD observations; a failed or empty update cannot be replaced by cached data. Non-daily commodity references are rejected in Data Audit.

No benchmark is fetched by QuantStats. Alpha, beta, information ratio and portfolio risk contributions are unavailable because an approved benchmark and portfolio mandate have not been supplied. The constant annual hurdle is a configured research assumption, not an observed currency-specific risk-free series.

Comparison rules

Trailing windows share the earliest last observation date across accepted OHLC instruments. Each retains all its native daily observations within the window; exchange holidays are not filled or removed to force a shared calendar. An instrument needs OOS history covering the window start. Actual first/last dates and counts are disclosed. Full OOS periods can differ. KPI medians summarize independent strategies; they are not portfolio returns.

MK Quantitative Research · EODHD reference-series research · Cash indices are not directly tradable. FX conversion, short borrow, carry and futures rolls are not modeled. Strategy results are backtests on observed data, not synthetic market observations or realized portfolio returns.
